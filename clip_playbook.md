# Clip style playbook (built 3 Oct 2026)

Source: 141 short-form clips that blew up between mid-August and 3 October 2026, gathered from TikTok,
Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. 96 come from clip pages (people clipping someone else's podcast or
stream) and 45 from official show or creator accounts. TikTok/Instagram picks are vidIQ outliers with
1M+ views that beat their own account's median (often 10x to 5000x). YouTube picks are Shorts with
300K+ views found through YouTube search. Full records: clip_style_db.json.

Counts below say how often a style shows up among these winners. They are not a ranking of which style
earns more, because every clip here already did well.

## The styles

### F2 Word-pop captions (67 of 141, the default)
- Tight 9:16 crop on whoever is talking, cut to the listener for reactions.
- 1 to 3 words on screen at a time, heavy condensed caps (Anton, Impact, Bebas, Montserrat ExtraBold),
  white with a thick black outline, centred around 55 to 65 percent of the height.
- Key words coloured: money and numbers green or red, names or punchlines yellow. Swearing censored (SH*T).
- Clip pages on YouTube: about 16 to 33 s. TikTok clip pages: about 25 to 68 s.
- Examples: Theo Rogan Shorts (1.9M, 1.8M), Caleb Hammer's own Shorts (2.9M, 2.7M), clipsoftogurt on TikTok (1.5M).

### F1 Headline label (33 of 141, most clip-page heavy: 31 of 33 are clip pages)
- A white rounded box with black bold text, written like a story headline:
  "[Name] was SHOCKED / SPEECHLESS / in DISBELIEF after [specific thing] [emoji]".
- Sits above the speaker's head, at the seam of a split, or above a letterboxed clip on black or blurred fill.
- Usually no word-by-word captions. Short: 10 to 30 s. Often loops.
- Streamer clips carry the Kick logo and kick.com/<streamer> bar at the bottom.
- Examples: slushymj "you're getting married to a women" 12.7M, kick_afterdark (Pinkchyu on N3on's stream) 8.9M,
  theclipstudiox "Fousey was SHOCKED after he found out N3on got PAID $3,500,000" 3.1M, crystal clips (Sneako) 2.5M.

### F3 Stacked split (35 of 141)
- Two (sometimes three) panels stacked. Top: the speaker or reactor. Bottom: the person or thing being talked about
  (photo, fight footage, the other caller, a text-message screenshot).
- Captions sit on the seam between panels.
- Examples: Aurafy (Lacy and Kai Cenat) 1.9M, HasanAbi reacts, Club Shay Shay with a photo of the person discussed,
  DamageCard (Jake Paul on top, the fight underneath).

### F5 Visual inserts (21 of 141)
- Pop-in photo cards, red arrows and circles, emoji props, meme cutaways, sound labels like *LAUGHING* or *MERCEDES*.
- Shows what the speaker is talking about the moment they say it.
- Examples: Impaulsive (red arrow on Logan's hairline plus a Wayne's World cutaway), Flagrant (photo cards of a
  praying mantis), Nansend Sports (circles on the fighter during a breakdown).

### F4 Title bar (17 of 141, mostly official shows)
- One headline stays on screen for the whole clip, as a top or mid band, often with the show logo or a news-style
  lower third. Used on longer clips (60 to 180 s) so people who join halfway still know what it is about.
- Examples: Club Shay Shay, The Diary Of A CEO, PBD Podcast, Breezy Politics, Caleb Hammer on TikTok 8.5M.

### F6 Viewer-voice bubble (8 of 141)
- A chat or comment bubble in the viewer's voice: "no way he actually has a family too!! 😱",
  "There ain't no way, why bro go on the interview and actually said that". Or a relatable meme caption over an
  interview ("Me defending my friend in any and every situation", 6.7M). Invites people to comment.

### F7 Recap / hype edit (6 of 141)
- Multi-angle punch-ins, coloured kinetic text, music; year tags ("2025"); before/after recreations
  (a real Speed moment on top, a Minecraft remake underneath, 3.3M). Mostly for creator/challenge content.
- Example: MrBeast Clips "MrBeast Couldn't Handle Mike Tyson" 11.1M (yellow year tag, yellow captions, meme outro).

### F8 Ranking / countdown (3 of 141)
- Numbered list that fills in as the clip plays ("Ranking the funniest Rizzler moments", UFC "Top 3 finishes").

### F9 Doc box (2 of 141, both big)
- Black canvas, the clip in a rounded 16:9 box, a top title with one highlighted word, B-roll cutaways.
- The Founder Feed "He Made $1.3M Buying a $65M Building" 5.6M. Fits money and business podcasts.

### F10 Comment-goal CTA (1 of 141)
- "25k comments on this post and Dana White will announce ..." Turns comments into the point of the post.

## Hooks that recur on clip pages
- Name + emotion in caps + specific detail + emoji: "Howie Mandel was SPEECHLESS after hearing ...".
- A question the clip answers: "MJ or Bron? 🤨", "What does the average Chick-fil-A franchise owner make".
- A big specific number: "$3,500,000", "$20M", "$800,000 per year".
- Open on the strongest spoken line itself, no setup ("I GREW UP IN ATLANTA ...", "the dumbest question I've ever asked myself").

## Rules that decide whether a clip earns
- Instagram, since 30 Apr 2026: accounts that mostly repost are left out of Explore and recommendations. Adding a
  caption or watermark alone does not count as original. Transform the clip, add commentary or edits, or use the
  Collab invite when a campaign gives one.
- YouTube: clips need significant original value (commentary, editing that tells a story). Mass-produced,
  barely-different uploads get the whole channel demonetised.
- Campaign rejections: missing hashtag or credit, wrong source footage, banned edits, duplicate posts,
  unlinked accounts, budget already spent. Post in the first 48 hours of a campaign.
- Pay seen in 2026: Whop Content Rewards $0.20 to $6 per 1,000 views (about $1 typical); MrBeast's Vyro about
  $3 per 1,000; N3on pays clippers $40 to $50 per 100,000.
